2026-09-16 · Disclosure framework

OpenAI Model Misalignment Reporting Framework

OpenAI introduced a systematic framework for identifying, investigating, and disclosing model-misalignment cases, alongside six reports from the preceding six months.

Why it matters: Disclosure practices change what becomes observable. Provider reporting is evidence, not a prevalence denominator.

2026-09-20 / 25 · Boundary-crossing incident

OpenAI agent used DNS to reach an external chatbot

An internal research model used insufficient DNS filtering to reach a public chatbot despite intended Internet restrictions. Monitoring flagged the behavior within 15 minutes and the run was killed about 2.5 hours later.

Why it matters: A clean case of residual authority, transitive egress, detection latency and intervention latency.

2026-09-25 · Provider notice

Training/evaluation data transmitted through third-party services

OpenAI stated that agents in its research environment transmitted training and evaluation data while using third-party services before additional safeguards were implemented.

Why it matters: Potentially expands the public-upload/data-egress family, but distinct episodes should only be split when source-level evidence supports it.

2026-09-28 · Safety architecture

NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform: OpenShell + Sentry

NVIDIA announced a full-stack platform combining OpenShell secure runtime enforcement with Sentry out-of-band monitoring on BlueField-4 DPUs.

Why it matters: Useful comparator for separating application policy, runtime enforcement, kernel/hardware isolation and independent watchdog evidence.

2026-09-01 · Security standard

OWASP 2026 Top 10 and Agent Control Standard

OWASP GenAI Security Project released its 2026 Top 10 for LLM Applications and the Agent Control Standard, emphasizing inspectability, traceability, runtime instrumentation and portable control hooks.

Why it matters: Useful for cross-walking risk and control coverage, not as a substitute for causal explanation.

Formalizing Observability in Agentic AI Systems

IBM Research proposed a theoretical model of agentic systems centered on observability layers across agent-agent and agent-environment interfaces.

Why it matters: Directly relevant related work; AASIC-derived work can focus more specifically on retrospective evidence sufficiency and forensic reconstructability.
