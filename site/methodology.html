Methodology

Evidence before labels.

Primary causal chain

attempt → boundary crossing → observable action → external effect → impact → harm

Attribution chain

evidence → causal attribution → actor attribution

The finer v0.5 stages are optional because historical public evidence is often incomplete. AASIC does not fill missing stages by inference.

Unit of analysis

A supportable causal episode, not a headline, target count, tool call, action count or press mention.

Separation rule

Human-directed malicious use of agents is documented as related evidence but is not silently mixed with autonomous/misaligned-agent episodes.

AASIC · evidence-bounded research corpus · counts are not prevalence estimates.
