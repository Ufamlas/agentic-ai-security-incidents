# Independent Incident Reports

This directory contains standalone, publication-oriented reports for each AASIC record.

The compact files under `cases/` are evidence cards. The files under `reports/` are independent technical reports intended to be read, cited, reviewed, or exported without opening the rest of the corpus.

## Report classes

- `confirmed/` — confirmed real-world incidents and evaluation-originated events with real external effects.
- `partial-attribution/` — real-world activity with incomplete causal or actor attribution.
- `controlled/` — controlled boundary crossings without documented external harm.
- `experimental/` — controlled misalignment and simulation-only evidence.

## Required report sections

Every report contains:
1. identification and classification;
2. executive summary;
3. system and operational context;
4. assigned objective and expected boundary;
5. observable effect chain;
6. confirmed facts;
7. effect and harm assessment;
8. evidence inventory;
9. causal and actor-attribution assessment;
10. forensic reconstructability;
11. unresolved questions and evidence gaps;
12. relevance to AASIC research lines;
13. source list.

## Epistemic rule

A report must not silently convert a provider interpretation, model self-report, chain-of-thought statement, press claim, or plausible inference into a confirmed fact.
